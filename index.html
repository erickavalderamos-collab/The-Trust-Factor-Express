Chapter 07

The Bigger Picture

PROTECT DATA · PROTECT TRUST
🗂️
🔒
🌐
Whether cardholder data is printed, stored locally, or transmitted over a public network, merchants have a role in keeping it secure.
Limit retention

Store cardholder data only when necessary for business, legal, and/or regulatory purposes.

Purge unnecessary data

Data stored unnecessarily should be purged at least quarterly.

Why does compliance matter?

⚖️Litigation
💸Fines and penalties
🚫Removal or reduction of card services
