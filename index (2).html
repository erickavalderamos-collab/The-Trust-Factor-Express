What is the rule?

Tap each principle to reveal what protecting confidential information means.

Use Company confidential information only for legitimate business purposes. Never use it for personal benefit or for the benefit of anyone other than Express.
Share confidential information internally or externally only on a need-to-know basis and when there is a legitimate business reason.
Do not share Company confidential information outside Express unless proper authorization is obtained under Company policy and the third party has entered into an NDA approved by the Legal Department.
The responsibility to protect confidential information also applies to information received from third parties under non-disclosure agreements.
Your obligation to protect Company confidential information continues even after your employment with Express ends.
Return all materials containing Company confidential information on or before your last day. Never take or use confidential information or materials from a previous employer or competitor.

The Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard contains 12 requirements designed to protect cardholder data. All merchants that accept payment cards must adhere to the standard.

Chapter 07

The Bigger Picture

07 / 08
PROTECT DATA · PROTECT TRUST
🗂️
🔒
🌐
Whether cardholder data is printed, stored locally, or transmitted over a public network to a remote server or service provider, merchants have a role in keeping it secure.

Keep only what is necessary.

Limit retention

Store cardholder data only when necessary for business needs. Retention time must be limited to business, legal, and/or regulatory purposes.

Purge unnecessary data

Data stored unnecessarily should be purged at least quarterly.

Why does compliance matter?

⚖️
Litigation
💸
Fines and penalties
🚫
Removal or reduction of payment card services

Failure to meet PCI DSS can expose a merchant to these consequences. Breaches can also harm company reputation and customer relationships.

Remember: It costs less to comply with PCI DSS requirements than to manage a data breach.
